# v6 Phase 4 — Interactive Research Instruments

Phase 4 upgrades the argument map and three research instruments without changing the scientific claim status of the project.

## Delivered

- Argument map now has three registry-backed representations: spatial map, dependency list, and semantic outline.
- Spatial graph highlights selected, upstream, and downstream structure with restrained state-driven motion.
- Assumption Lab adds a live dependency-state strip, local warnings, and clearer active/blocked premise states.
- Spectrum Lab adds a quantitative mixture-weight display, supplied versus lab-linked Δ modes, a rescaling schematic axis, and a local T0 provenance ledger.
- RG Transfer adds a signed zero-centered budget visualization for gross transfer, defect subtraction, and net bound.
- Positive, zero, negative, conditional, and blocked states use distinct text and color semantics; positive never means “proved.”
- All new motion is tied to a real state change and respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Spectrum mode is now part of the v6 scenario serialization (`sm=supplied|lab`).

## Scientific integrity

No new Yang–Mills theorem is asserted. The Spectrum view remains schematic and computes no Hamiltonian eigenvalues. The RG view remains a T0 single-step arithmetic budget and does not represent an RG trajectory. Lab-linked Δ is a conditional visualization of the lab scalar result, not a physical spectral measurement.
